## About

Dr. Sambasivam Gnanasekaran is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computing and Data Science at Xiamen University Malaysia. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering. With over a decade of comprehensive experience spanning academia, research, and industry, his expertise encompasses Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning. He has authored and co-authored more than 35 peer-reviewed publications, including 14 SCIE/Scopus-indexed journal articles in Elsevier, IEEE, and Springer Nature. He is an active IEEE member and serves as a reviewer for several leading SCI-indexed journals and international conferences. He leads a funded research project under the XMUM Research Fund (XMUMRF) and previously served in academic leadership roles including Associate Dean.

### Research areas
- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Deep Learning
